## 3. Confirmed Programmes & Real-World Initiatives

### A. "Nurture Early Learning" — Government School & Anganwadi Infrastructure Revitalization
* **Locations Verified:** Peddagudem (Wanaparthy district) & Ibrahimpatnam mandal, Telangana.
* **Scope of Documented Work:**
  * Complete structural waterproofing of leaking concrete roofs/slabs to ensure safety during monsoons.
  * Installation of clean, resilient vitrified tile flooring replacing broken or uneven surfaces.
  * Ergonomic dual student desks and seating benches to elevate learning from floor sitting to dignified classrooms.
  * Clean classroom storage units and steel cupboards for books and teaching aids.
  * Vibrant educational wall art, murals, and chalkboard/whiteboard renewals.
  * Distribution of comprehensive English-Telugu dictionaries, stationery sets, and learning kits for rural pupils.
* **Verification Status:** Fully Confirmed with photographic documentation of before/after school conditions and opening ceremonies.

### B. Civic Safety & Law Enforcement Infrastructure Partnerships
* **Locations Verified:** KPHB Metro Station & Paparayudu Nagar Junction, Kukatpally (Hyderabad).
* **Scope of Documented Work:**
  * **KPHB Metro Police Outpost:** Built and sponsored a dedicated permanent police outpost booth directly beneath the busy KPHB Metro Station in partnership with Cyberabad Police (Kukatpally Division), aimed at curbing street harassment, ensuring women's safety during late transit hours, and providing police visibility.
  * **Paparayudu Nagar Traffic & Road Safety Modifications:** Fabricated and deployed custom heavy-duty steel safety barricades and traffic-calming guidance to eliminate dangerous unorganized parking and resolve recurring traffic bottlenecks.
  * **"Arrive Alive" Campaign Support:** Organized public helmet distribution drives and awareness programs in alignment with the Telangana State road safety mission, providing certified safety helmets to two-wheeler riders.
* **Verification Status:** Fully Confirmed through Cyberabad Police public events, joint inaugurations with police commissioners/inspectors, and press photos.

### C. Critical Medical Care & Healthcare Lifelines
* **Scope of Documented Work:**
  * Financial assistance and direct hospital bill sponsorships for families unable to afford emergency operations, oncology treatments, or life-saving pediatric surgeries.
  * Sustained medical assurance: Ongoing assistance with essential medicines for destitute seniors and chronic patients.
* **Verification Status:** Confirmed via verified case announcements and community outreach posts. Individual patient identities protected in accordance with privacy ethics.

### D. Community Relief & Grassroots Welfare
* **Scope of Documented Work:**
  * Mass free clean drinking water distribution and hydration stalls during high-density public gatherings (notably Ganesh Nimajjanam at IDL Lake, Kukatpally).
  * Direct relief, clothes, and sustenance distribution during seasonal vulnerabilities and festivals.
* **Verification Status:** Confirmed with documented public service camps.
